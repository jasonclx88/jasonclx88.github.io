ABOUT LIANG CHEN

A life in research.

Associate Professor at Sun Yat-Sen University. Researcher, collaborator, and educator.

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-Sen University, Guangzhou, China.

My research includes graph learning, trustworthy AI, large language models, and recommendation systems.

I received my Ph.D. in 2015 and Bachelor’s degree in 2009 from Zhejiang University, where I worked with the Advanced Computing and System Laboratory (CCNT), College of Computer Science and Technology.

I graduated with honors from Zhejiang University and received an honors degree from Chu Kochen College.
